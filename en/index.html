Customs Capability

The rules changed.
Only prepared logistics keeps moving.

Korea's dedicated e-commerce customs platform, in force from 2026, sharply tightened importer verification and operator registration. We have built the matching verification and alerting workflow to keep clearance delays to a minimum.

Customs capability in detail →
  1. Importer details verified in advanceWe check the personal customs clearance code, name, phone number and address against each other before goods leave the warehouse.
  2. Real-time alerts on unapproved shipmentsApproval status is read at the moment of scanning, cutting the time held cargo sits in the warehouse.
  3. E-commerce operator code managementWe handle operator registration under the amended Customs Act and the dedicated declaration and manifest entries.
  4. Licensed customs brokers and specialist toolsWe work with clearance specialists to reduce risk in tariff classification and requirement checks.
